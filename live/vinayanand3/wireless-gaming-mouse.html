136 convex parts, re-projected and re-shaded every frame and painted in an order solved from separating planes. The top shell is an intersection of fitted spheres: each panel is one sphere cut by its neighbours' radical planes, so the panels are curved and meet along exact character lines. Dimensions and internals are a plausible concept, not a production teardown.Dashed coloured lines are conceptual signal, light and power overlays drawn over the model; they are not modelled parts. Wireless is shown schematically.
